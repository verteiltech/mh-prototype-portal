MH NDC — The Future of
Distribution

Richer content, direct from MH

MH NDC lets appointed agents access richer fare content, ancillaries, and merchandising directly through their booking tool of choice — no redirection required.

Benefits for You

★
Improve competitiveness

Access exclusive fares and offers not available via legacy channels.

★
Boost agent productivity

Fewer manual steps between search, price, and ticketing.

★
Enhance customer satisfaction

Faster quotes and more relevant ancillary offers for travellers.

Flexible API Features

Dynamic Pricing
  • Real-time fare rules
  • Bundled ancillaries
Rich Content
  • Seat maps & media
  • Branded fares
Servicing
  • Self-service changes
  • Automated refunds

MH Portal — New Booking Portal Now Live

Share this article

We are pleased to announce that the new MH Portal booking experience is now live for all appointed travel agents. This update introduces a streamlined flight search flow, richer fare content, and faster order retrieval.

Agents can continue to access existing bookings made through the previous system — no re-registration is required. Training materials and a quick-start guide are available under Agent Resources > Training & Certification.

Should you encounter any issues during the transition, please reach out to the MH trade support desk via the Contact Us page.
